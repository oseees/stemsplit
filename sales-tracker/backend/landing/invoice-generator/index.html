What to put on an invoice in Nigeria

  • Your business name and phone number, so the customer knows who to pay and how to reach you.
  • The customer's name.
  • An invoice number and date. Numbering in order (INV-001, INV-002…) makes it easy to find later.
  • Each item with quantity and price in Naira, and the total.
  • How to pay: bank name, account number and account name.

Invoice or receipt: which one do I send?

Send an invoice before you're paid: it asks the customer to pay. Send a receipt after you're paid: it proves the money came in. If a customer paid part, the SalesPal app makes a receipt that shows the balance still owed.

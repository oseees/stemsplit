What is a proforma invoice?

A proforma invoice is a price estimate in invoice form that you send before a sale is final. Companies, schools and government offices in Nigeria often need one to get payment approved or to open a purchase order. It lists exactly what the customer will get and what it will cost, but it is not a demand for payment and not a receipt.

Proforma invoice vs invoice vs quotation

  • Quotation: "Here's my price" — for the customer to compare and decide.
  • Proforma invoice: "Here's exactly what you'll be billed" — for the customer to approve and arrange payment.
  • Invoice: "Please pay" — sent once the sale is confirmed.

What to put on a proforma invoice

  • Your business name, phone number and the words "Proforma Invoice".
  • The customer or company name, a proforma number and date.
  • Each item with quantity and price in Naira, and the total.
  • Your bank details, so payment can be arranged once it's approved.

Frequently asked questions

What is a proforma invoice used for in Nigeria?

It is sent before a sale is final so the customer can approve the purchase, get funds released, or raise a purchase order. It shows exactly what will be supplied and the total cost.

Is a proforma invoice the same as an invoice?

No. A proforma is an estimate for approval and is not a request for payment. Once the customer agrees, you send a normal invoice, and a receipt after they pay.

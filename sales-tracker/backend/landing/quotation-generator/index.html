What should a quotation include?

  • Your business name and phone number, and the word "Quotation".
  • Who it's for, a quotation number and the date.
  • Every item or service with quantity and price in Naira, and the total.
  • Anything that affects the price: delivery, installation, or how long your price holds (add it as an item or in your message).

Tips to win more quotes

  • Send it fast. The first clear quote on WhatsApp often wins.
  • Itemise. A breakdown looks more trustworthy than one lump sum.
  • Follow up after two days. Many "we'll get back to you" quotes are won by the follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a quotation and an invoice?

A quotation tells the customer what something will cost before they agree. An invoice asks them to pay after the sale is agreed.

Is this quotation generator free?

Yes. Make and download as many quotations as you want, with no sign-up.

Can I send the quotation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Tap Send on WhatsApp for a text quotation, or Download PDF and attach the file in the chat.

Can I turn the quotation into an invoice?

Yes. Change Document to Invoice and it becomes an invoice with the same items. In the SalesPal app, a saved sale creates the invoice automatically.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask

    Is SalesPal free?

    Yes. The free plan lets you track sales and expenses, see your profit, and send up to 15 invoices a month with one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders. Pro is ₦3,000/month (or ₦30,000/year) for unlimited invoices, multiple shops, and AI business insights.

    Do I need to download it from the App Store or Play Store?

    No. SalesPal is a web app — open salespal.online on your phone and add it to your home screen, and it opens like any other app, even offline. On Android you can also install the app directly from the site.

    Can I record who owes me money?

    Yes. SalesPal keeps a debt book: every customer who owes you, how much, and for how long — sorted by the biggest debt. You can mark a customer paid, send a statement, or send a friendly WhatsApp reminder in one tap.

    Can I send invoices and receipts on WhatsApp?

    Yes. You can create a professional invoice or receipt in a few taps and share it straight into a WhatsApp chat as an image or PDF, so your customer sees it immediately.

    Does it work with a poor or no internet connection?

    Yes. You can record sales even with no network — they save on your phone and sync automatically once you're back online.

    Does it use Naira, and does it work in Nigeria?

    Yes. SalesPal is built for Nigerian businesses and works in Naira by default. You can change the currency symbol in settings if you sell in another country.

    Is my business data safe?

    Your data is private to your account and protected by your password. Only you (and any staff logins you create) can see your numbers, and staff never see your profit or costs.

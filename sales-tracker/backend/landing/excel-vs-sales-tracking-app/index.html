Excel vs a sales tracking app: which should you use?

Excel is free and flexible, and plenty of businesses start there. But a spreadsheet on a laptop and a busy shop on a phone don't always mix. Here's an honest comparison.

When Excel is fine

  • You record a few sales a week and sit at a laptop anyway.
  • You're comfortable with formulas and like building your own sheets.
  • You need a very custom report that no app gives you.

When an app is better

  • You sell all day and need to record in seconds, on your phone.
  • You give credit and need to track and remind people who owe.
  • You want profit, not just sales, without building formulas.
  • Someone else sells for you and you want a record you can trust.

Questions people ask

Is Excel good for sales records?

Excel works for small volumes if you are comfortable with formulas and use a laptop. For busy shops recording sales all day on a phone, an app is faster and less error-prone.

What is the best way to record daily sales?

Record every sale as it happens with the item, quantity, price and whether it was paid, and record expenses too. An app does the adding up and shows your profit automatically.

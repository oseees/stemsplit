The daily sales record book, now on your phone

The paper sales book has kept Nigerian shops running for years — but it tears, goes missing, and never adds itself up. The same habit on your phone keeps every entry safe and does the maths for you.

SalesPal · updated August 2026

Why the paper book falls short

  • It gets lost or damaged. One spilt drink or a missing booklet and weeks of records are gone.
  • It doesn't add up. You still have to total it by hand to know how you did — so most people don't.
  • It hides your profit. A sales book shows what came in, not what you kept after cost and expenses.
  • Only you can read it. No backup, no way to check last month quickly.

What a phone-based sales record gives you

  • Every sale, saved. Record it in a few taps as you sell; nothing to lose.
  • Instant totals. Today, this week, this month — added up automatically.
  • Real profit, not just takings. Enter cost prices once and see what you actually keep.
  • Always with you. It's on your phone, and it works even when the network is down.
💡 Keep the habit, drop the paper. The end-of-day "what did I sell?" routine is the same — it's just faster and it remembers everything for you.

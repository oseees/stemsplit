How to send invoices and receipts on WhatsApp

In Nigeria, business happens on WhatsApp. A clear invoice or receipt in the chat makes you look established and gets you paid faster than a handwritten note ever will.

SalesPal · updated August 2026

Why WhatsApp beats a paper receipt

  • It's where your customer already is. No printing, no photos of scribbled paper.
  • It's proof. A dated invoice showing items, quantities and total settles disputes before they start.
  • It looks professional. Your business name on a clean invoice signals that you're serious.
  • It speeds up payment. Put your account number or a payment link on the invoice and there's nothing stopping the customer paying now.

Image or PDF — which to send?

Both work, but they behave differently in a chat:

  • An image (PNG/JPG) previews right inside the WhatsApp conversation, so the customer sees it immediately without tapping. Best for quick confirmation.
  • A PDF arrives as a file to open. It's better when the customer needs to keep or forward a formal document.
💡 Tip: for everyday sales, send the image — people are far more likely to look at something that shows up in the chat than a file they have to open.

What a good invoice includes

  1. Your business name (and phone/logo if you have one).
  2. An invoice number and the date.
  3. The items, quantities and prices, with a clear total.
  4. How to pay — your account details or a payment link.
  5. For a receipt: the amount paid and any balance remaining.

How to send a receipt to your customer on WhatsApp

A customer pays, and asks for a receipt. Scribbling on a paper booklet looks unprofessional and gets lost; typing it out by hand takes too long. The easy way is to send a clean receipt straight to their WhatsApp — as an image they can open and keep.

SalesPal · updated August 2026

Why a proper receipt matters

A receipt is proof of what was paid, when, and for what. It builds trust, cuts "I already paid you" disputes, and makes your business look organised. Sent on WhatsApp — where your customer already is — it lands instantly and they can save or forward it.

The slow ways (and why they don't scale)

  • Paper booklet: easy to lose, hard to read, no copy for you.
  • Typing it out in chat: slow, looks casual, and easy to get the numbers wrong.
  • A PDF: better, but some phones fumble opening PDFs — your customer shouldn't have to hunt for a reader.
💡 An image receipt (PNG/JPG) opens the moment it lands, on any phone, with no app or reader needed — the smoothest option for customers.

The fast way, step by step

  1. Record the sale or the payment you received.
  2. Your app generates a professional receipt image in Naira, with your business name on it.
  3. Tap Share and pick the customer's WhatsApp chat — the receipt sends as an image.
  4. Done. They open it instantly; you keep a matching record automatically.

Kippa alternative: where to keep your business records now

Kippa's bookkeeping app stopped working in early 2024, and many Nigerian business owners lost access to their records overnight. If you're starting again, here's how to rebuild quickly — and what to look for this time.

SalesPal · updated September 2026

What happened to Kippa?

Kippa was a popular Nigerian bookkeeping app for small businesses. In 2023 it shut down its KippaPay agency banking service, and from early 2024 the bookkeeping app became inaccessible. Users reported losing access to their sales, debtor and invoice records. The company has since been reported as inactive.

What to look for in a replacement

  • A company that's active and answering. Check it's being updated and that you can reach a person.
  • Backups. Ask how your records are protected. SalesPal backs up its database off-site every night.
  • Copies you keep. In SalesPal you can download all your records — sales, payments, expenses, products and customers — as spreadsheets any time, from Settings → Your records. If you ever leave, your data leaves with you.
  • Built for how you work: Naira, WhatsApp, and working without network.

What to put on a receipt

  • Your business name and phone number.
  • The customer's name and the date they paid.
  • A receipt number (RCT-001, RCT-002…) so you can find it if there's ever a dispute.
  • What they bought, with quantity and price in Naira.
  • The amount paid and how: cash, transfer or POS.

Why give receipts at all?

A receipt settles arguments before they start. When a customer says "I paid you already", you both have the same record. It also makes a small business look serious, and customers come back to sellers they trust.

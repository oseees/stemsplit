When Bumpa is the better choice

  • You want a full online store website with your own domain.
  • You sell across many channels and run SMS/email marketing campaigns.
  • You have several locations and bigger teams.

When SalesPal is the better choice

  • You mainly sell in a shop, on WhatsApp or in the market, and want clean records.
  • You want to know your real profit and who owes you — fast.
  • You'd rather start free and pay ₦3,000 a month only when you need more.

What SalesPal includes

  • Sales and real profit in Naira — each sale carries its cost, so you see profit, not just turnover.
  • Invoices and receipts on WhatsApp, including part payments with the balance shown.
  • Debt book — who owes you, how much, with one-tap WhatsApp reminders.
  • Stock with low-stock alerts, and reorders to your supplier on WhatsApp.
  • Expenses, so profit is after costs.
  • Attendant logins — staff record sales without seeing your profit.
  • Works offline and syncs when you're back online.
  • Record by chat or voice — type "sold 3 bags of rice to Ada, she paid 20k" and it's recorded.

Questions people ask

Is Bumpa free?

Bumpa offers a free trial. Paid plans start from ₦5,000 per month, billed quarterly, according to Bumpa's pricing page in September 2026.

What is a cheaper alternative to Bumpa?

SalesPal has a free plan for sales, expenses and profit, and Pro costs ₦3,000 per month or ₦30,000 per year. It focuses on records, invoices and debts rather than a full online store.

Does SalesPal have an online store like Bumpa?

SalesPal Pro gives you a shareable order link where customers order from your in-stock products. It is not a full website with a custom domain; if you need that, Bumpa is the stronger choice.

Is SalesPal free?

Free plan: sales, expenses and profit, up to 20 products and 15 invoices a month. Pro: ₦3,000/month or ₦30,000/year.

Why turnover isn't the number that matters

A busy day feels great, but a shop can sell ₦500,000 in a month and keep almost nothing. What matters is profit — and you only see it when sales, cost of goods and expenses are all recorded. SalesPal does that maths for you, live, so you always know where you stand.

💡 The fastest way to grow is usually to push your high-profit products, not just to sell more. A sales tracker that shows profit tells you which ones those are.

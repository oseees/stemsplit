What is a waybill?

A waybill (also called a delivery note) is the list of goods that travels with a delivery. It shows who sent it, who it's going to, and exactly what's inside — so the receiver can check nothing is missing and sign that it arrived in good condition. It usually has no prices on it.

What to put on a waybill

  • Your business name and phone number, and a waybill number.
  • The receiver's name (and address or bus park, in the item list or message).
  • Every item and its quantity.
  • A space for the receiver to sign and date on delivery — this generator adds it automatically.
Sending goods interstate by bus? Take a photo of the signed waybill when the goods are collected. It's your proof if anything goes missing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a waybill and an invoice?

A waybill lists the goods being delivered and their quantities, and is signed on delivery. An invoice lists prices and asks for payment.

Does a waybill show prices?

Usually not. This generator leaves prices off the waybill; switch Document to Invoice if you also need to bill the customer.

# Broiler Weight Gain and 6-Week Targets (Nigeria)

> ℹ️ **Nigeria context.** Targets are typical for good modern broiler strains (Ross/Cobb-type) under good management; your hatchery strain, feed quality and the weather will shift them. Use NAFDAC-registered feeds and check the label for crude protein (CP) and energy. There is **NO legal or effective hormone** for poultry growth — weight comes from feed quality, management and disease control. Never use banned drugs or antibiotic growth promoters; observe the meat withdrawal period on any medicine.

**Goal:** reach a healthy market weight (about **2.0–2.5 kg live by 6 weeks**) with a low feed conversion ratio (**FCR ~1.6–1.8**, i.e. ~1.6–1.8 kg feed per 1 kg gain).

**Typical live-weight targets (as-hatched, good conditions):**
- Week 1: ~150–200 g
- Week 2: ~400–500 g
- Week 3: ~750–950 g
- Week 4: ~1.1–1.5 kg
- Week 5: ~1.6–2.0 kg
- Week 6: ~2.0–2.5 kg

Many Nigerian farms reach only ~1.6–2.0 kg at 6 weeks; closing the gap is almost always about feed and management, not "boosters".

**Feed phases (typical):**
- **Starter** (day 0–10/14): high protein, ~22–23% CP, energy ~2,950–3,050 kcal/kg ME. Crumble form.
- **Grower** (≈ day 11–24): ~21% CP, ~3,050–3,150 kcal/kg ME.
- **Finisher** (≈ day 25 to sale): ~19% CP, ~3,150–3,200 kcal/kg ME.

Give feed and clean water free-choice (ad libitum). Total feed to 6 weeks is typically ~3.5–4 kg/bird.

**Safe levers to boost weight gain (in order of impact):**
1. **Feed quality and quantity** — buy fresh, properly formulated feed with the right CP/energy; never dilute or "stretch" feed. Mouldy, aflatoxin-contaminated or adulterated feed is a leading cause of poor growth in Nigeria.
2. **Brooding temperature** — ~33–35°C under the brooder in week 1, reduced ~2–3°C per week to ~24°C. Cold or overheated chicks eat less and grow poorly.
3. **Water** — cool, clean, unlimited water; birds drink roughly 1.6–2× the weight of feed eaten. Any water shortage instantly cuts growth.
4. **Feeder and drinker space** — enough so all birds can eat at once; refill often. Use ~23 hours light on day 1, then include a short dark period.
5. **Stocking density** — avoid overcrowding; heat and competition stunt growth.
6. **Health** — control coccidiosis and worms (follow your prevention/vaccination programme) and treat respiratory disease early; sick birds do not gain weight.
7. **Heat management** — in hot weather feed during the cooler morning/evening hours and add vitamins/electrolytes to the water.

**Common reasons broilers miss the 6-week target (Nigeria):** poor or adulterated feed, mouldy/aflatoxin feed, underfeeding to cut cost, heat stress, dirty or scarce water, poor brooding, overcrowding, and subclinical coccidiosis/worms/CRD.

**Confirmatory check:** weigh a sample of 20–30 birds each week and compare to the targets above to catch a shortfall early. If birds look sick or growth suddenly drops, run a health diagnosis.

**When to call a vet/nutritionist:** if good feed and management still miss targets, or growth is very uneven, ask a vet or poultry nutritionist to check flock health and assay the feed.

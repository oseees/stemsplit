# Poultry Vaccination Schedule (Nigeria — typical)

> ℹ️ **Nigeria context.** A typical programme — exact vaccines, ages and routes vary by hatchery, vaccine brand and the diseases common in your area. Confirm with your vet/vaccine supplier, vaccinate only healthy birds, and keep the cold chain. Good vaccination is the cheapest way to prevent the deadliest diseases (Newcastle, Gumboro, Marek's).

**Broilers:**
- Day 1 — Marek's disease (at hatchery, injection).
- Day 7–9 — Gumboro / IBD, 1st (drinking water).
- Day 14 — Newcastle (Lasota) ± infectious bronchitis, 1st (drinking water / eye drop).
- Day 18–21 — Gumboro / IBD, 2nd (drinking water).
- Day 24–28 — Newcastle (Lasota), 2nd (drinking water).
- Week 4 — Fowl pox (wing-web), only where fowl pox is common.

**Layers / pullets:**
- Day 1 — Marek's disease (at hatchery, injection).
- Day 7 — Newcastle (Hitchner B1 / Lasota) + infectious bronchitis, 1st (eye drop / drinking water).
- Day 14 — Gumboro / IBD, 1st (drinking water).
- Day 21 — Gumboro / IBD, 2nd (drinking water).
- Day 28 — Newcastle (Lasota) booster (drinking water).
- Week 6 — Fowl pox (wing-web) + Newcastle (Komarov / killed, injection).
- Week 8 — Fowl typhoid (injection), where endemic.
- Week 10–12 — Infectious coryza (injection), where endemic.
- Week 16–18 — Newcastle (killed) + infectious bronchitis + EDS before lay (injection); deworm around point-of-lay.
- In lay — Newcastle (Lasota) booster every ~8–12 weeks (drinking water) to keep immunity up.

**When to call a vet:** Routine — to tailor this programme to your area's disease pressure, your hatchery's day-old vaccinations, and the vaccine brands you can source locally.

**Sources / further reading:** Merck/MSD Veterinary Manual — "Vaccination Programs for Poultry"; standard Nigerian poultry vaccination guides.
